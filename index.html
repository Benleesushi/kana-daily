Support Privacy policy

Support

Kana Daily shows one row of Japanese kana each day, with spoken audio, quick quizzes, a weekly tracker, a daily reminder and a Home Screen widget. It works fully offline and needs no account.

Common questions

I can't hear any sound.

Kana Daily uses your iPhone's built-in Japanese voice. Check your volume, then go to Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › Japanese and download a voice such as Kyoko or Otoya. The Enhanced versions sound the most natural.

How do I change level, row order or appearance?

Tap the Level button at the top left. Pick Level 1, 2 or 3 there, or open Settings for Appearance (System, Light, Dark), Row order (In order or Random) and the daily reminder.

The reminder doesn't arrive.

Turn the reminder on in Kana Daily's Settings and allow notifications when asked. If you declined before, go to iPhone Settings › Notifications › KanaDaily and turn Allow Notifications on. The reminder is skipped on days you've already ticked off.

How do I add the widget?

Press and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and search for Kana Daily. Press and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget to set its level, script and row order so it matches the app.

How does the coverage map fill in?

Open Progress from the Level menu. Each kana on the map darkens every time you answer it correctly in the quiz.

Will I lose my progress?

Your ticks, streaks and quiz results are saved on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them, because nothing is stored online.

Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

Kana Daily does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your settings and study progress (level, script, ticked days, streaks, quiz scores and coverage map) are saved only on your iPhone using standard iOS storage. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

No accounts, ads or tracking

The app has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. It does not use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Audio

Pronunciation is played using the speech voice built into iOS, on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your iPhone. They are created on the device and no information is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in iPhone Settings at any time.

Widget

The Home Screen widget shows the day's row using the options you choose for it. It does not collect any data.

Children

Kana Daily is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
